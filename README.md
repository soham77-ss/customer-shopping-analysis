# Consumer Shopping Behavior Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview
This project performs an Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on consumer shopping behavior. The goal is to uncover purchasing patterns, demographic trends, and factors that influence customer decision-making to provide actionable business insights.

## 🎯 Objectives
*   **Customer Segmentation:** Understand which demographic groups drive the most revenue.
*   **Purchasing Patterns:** Analyze how variables like discounts, payment methods, and seasons affect shopping habits.
*   **Actionable Insights:** Translate data findings into strategic business recommendations.

## 🛠️ Tools & Libraries Used
*   **Python:** Data manipulation and analysis
*   **Pandas & NumPy:** Data cleaning and aggregation
*   **Matplotlib & Seaborn:** Data visualization
*   **Jupyter Notebook:** Interactive coding environment

## 📊 Key Insights & Findings
*   **Generational Payment Preferences:** There is a distinct split in preferred payment methods based on age; younger adults (aged 20-25) prefer using Cash, whereas customers over 35 favor PayPal[cite: 1].
*   **Demographic Product Trends:** Among female shoppers, the most popular purchased item is a Blouse, while Jeans are the least popular[cite: 1]. Meanwhile, the young adult demographic (20-25) most frequently purchases Sweaters and least frequently buys Shoes[cite: 1].
*   **Seasonal & Regional Peaks:** Spring is the most popular shopping season overall, and Montana is the leading state for the highest number of customer purchases[cite: 1].
*   **Customer Loyalty & Subscriptions:** Even though customers show solid retention with an average of about 25 previous purchases, the vast majority (2,847 out of 3,900 customers) do not currently have an active subscription, representing a potential area for business growth[cite: 1].
*   **Top Rated Products:** Gloves have the highest average review rating across the entire catalog (averaging 3.86 out of 5)[cite: 1].
